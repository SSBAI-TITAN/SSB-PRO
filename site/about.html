About

Hi, I'm Joseph Perez, CPA.

I founded SSB PRO CONSULTING to modernize the accounting function for small and mid-sized businesses, by putting a licensed CPA who understands AI directly in your corner, not a triaged ticket at a regional firm.

My approach is simple: I work alongside the systems you already rely on, adding intelligence, automation, and better workflows without ripping anything out. I care less about recommendations and more about execution, with solutions that get implemented, adopted, and move your numbers.
